# Hunter Lab — Museum-native research workflow

Hunter Lab is a custom workflow designed for Airdrop Museum, reached through Misi & Bounty. It connects four tools rather than adding unrelated navigation destinations.

- Hunter Compass builds a personal work session using saved exhibits, chain/focus preferences and a 15/30/60/120 minute budget. Active status contributes 20, chosen high priority 15, personal deadlines within three days 30 (overdue 40), and fewer than two dossier evidence categories 10. Name breaks ties. These internal ordering weights are work planning rules, never safety, eligibility or reward scores. Allocation follows the queue and permits a partial session.
- Bounty Brief records objective, deliverables, acceptance criteria, estimated effort, submission URL and self-reported delivery stage. Its six checks measure completeness only. Saving never submits a contribution externally.
- Proof Trail stores an annotated source, activity, submission or outcome together with the device timestamp and a snapshot of local progress. The first and latest checkpoints can be compared. Limits: 30 entries per exhibit and 200 total. URLs require HTTPS without credentials. Entries are personal records, not tamper-proof attestations or independently verified blockchain transactions.
- Research Pack exports the exhibit source, brief, checklist, transparency dossier, notes, trail and manual outcome ledger as Markdown. The export can contain private material and should be reviewed before sharing.

Data survives normalized JSON backup/import and follows the existing private state sync capability when authenticated sync is configured. Import deduplicates proof IDs; an existing substantive local brief takes precedence. Device storage and imports can be edited, so local XP and badges are personal progression only.

Two original quests: Research Receipt (70 local XP) and planned contribution (60 local XP). New badges: Trail Keeper and Contribution Architect. These do not issue tokens, monetary rewards or server referral XP.

The app's bundled catalog remains demo content. Hunter Lab does not claim to discover live bounty programs, verify submissions, predict allocations or activate Telegram authentication.
